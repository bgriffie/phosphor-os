    Arcade, Workout Log and Kitchen Log are built into the OS and open full-screen without leaving it. They share their data with the standalone versions on this phone. To return, press Home, or Back on an app's first page.

    How tiles open apps

    Mail opens Gmail (googlegmail://) and Maps opens Google Maps (comgooglemaps://). If an app isn't installed, nothing happens.

    Messages, Camera, FaceTime and any other app open through the Shortcuts app, because iOS only lets a link start a new message, not open Messages itself. Shortcut tiles are amber.

    • 1. In Shortcuts, tap +, add the "Open App" action and pick the app.
    • 2. Name it exactly what the tile says, for example "Open Camera".
    • 3. Tap the tile. iOS may ask once for permission.

    This is an app on your home screen, not a replacement for it. iOS doesn't let a web app list your apps, and it may reload when you come back, so everything is saved as you go.
