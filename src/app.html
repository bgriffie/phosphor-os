    Arcade, Workout Log and Kitchen Log are built into the OS and open full-screen without leaving it. They share their data with the standalone versions on this phone. To return, use Back on an app's home page or Phosphor OS in its ☰ menu.

    How tiles open apps

    Phone, Messages, Mail and Maps open through app links such as sms: or maps://. If an app isn't installed, nothing happens.

    Any other app (Camera, FaceTime, Settings) opens through the Shortcuts app. Shortcut tiles are amber.

    • 1. In Shortcuts, tap +, add the "Open App" action and pick the app.
    • 2. Name it exactly what the tile says, for example "Open Camera".
    • 3. Tap the tile. iOS may ask once for permission.

    This is an app on your home screen, not a replacement for it. iOS doesn't let a web app list your apps, and it may reload when you come back, so everything is saved as you go.
